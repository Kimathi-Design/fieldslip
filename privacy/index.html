Fieldslip

Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Your calculations

Fieldslip works without an account. Calculations run on this phone. The app remembers your latest calculator inputs, recent tools, and the choice to hide ads, in storage on the device.

Fieldslip has no server that receives your calculation values or QR content, and that content is not sent to advertising services.

Camera and QR codes

Camera access is optional. The camera starts only after you tap Scan a code and grant permission.

QR codes are read on this phone. Camera images are not saved or uploaded by Fieldslip. Scanned text becomes the QR tool’s input and is remembered like other tool inputs until you erase it.

Advertising

Fieldslip uses Google AdMob to display ads. The calculation tools work offline. Advertising needs an internet connection.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate approximate location. This information supports advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Fieldslip does not provide your entered calculation values or QR content to AdMob.

Google’s Privacy Policy

Your advertising choices

Where required, a privacy message lets you make choices before ads are requested. You can revisit available choices through Ad privacy choices in the app.

Android also provides controls for resetting or deleting your advertising ID. Those controls do not stop all other advertising-related data processing.

Storage, backup and deletion

Remembered inputs stay on this phone until you change them, erase them, or uninstall Fieldslip. They are left out of Android backup and device transfer.

Clear saved data removes remembered inputs, QR text, and recent tools. It does not erase information already processed by Google’s advertising services.

You can also remove the app’s data in Android settings. Open Fieldslip’s app information and choose Clear storage. That resets remembered inputs, recent tools, and the choice to hide ads.
